Research & Publications

BdSL-SPOTER: A Transformer-Based Framework for Bengali Sign Language Recognition

Accepted at ISVC'25 & published in Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS)

Novel transformer framework achieving 97.92% Top-1 accuracy for Bengali Sign Language Recognition. This research addresses the critical need for accessible communication tools for the deaf and hard-of-hearing community in Bangladesh. The framework leverages state-of-the-art transformer architectures combined with MediaPipe for robust hand tracking and gesture recognition.
